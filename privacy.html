TRACKERIST · PRIVACY INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026 · Applies to the unmodified Trackerist 3.2 client-side application.

Trackerist is a browser-based, offline-first attendance and finance tracker. This notice explains what information the app uses and where it is saved. It does not cover third-party modifications or separate services added by a site operator.

1. Information you enter

The app can store your chosen username, course codes and names, scheduled dates, room numbers, professor names, attendance statuses and notes, fine rates, bills, payments, semester settings, and preferences. You decide what details to enter.

2. Local account and password

Your chosen username and a salted password verifier (derived using PBKDF2-SHA-256) are stored in this browser's local storage. Trackerist does not intentionally save your plaintext password. Login is local to this browser profile; it is not a remote account or cloud authentication service. There is no password recovery service.

3. Where records are kept

The app saves records using your browser's localStorage, and caches app assets using the browser's service worker/Cache Storage for offline access. In this unmodified build, attendance and financial records are not automatically uploaded to a Trackerist application server or synchronized across devices. Data is not encrypted at rest by the app. Other people with access to your device/browser profile may be able to view it.

4. Hosting and third parties

If you open Trackerist on a hosted website (for example, GitHub Pages), your browser requests the site's files from that hosting service. That host or its infrastructure may process IP addresses, request details, and technical logs under its own policies. This notice cannot control or describe a particular hosting provider's practices. The unmodified Trackerist code does not include a built-in analytics or advertising SDK.

5. How information is used

Information entered into the app is used locally to display schedules, record attendance, calculate event fines, show bills/payments, and generate summaries and reports. Academic class absences are not automatically fined. Event fines apply to the individual AM/PM slots you mark Absent.

6. Exporting and sharing

You can create JSON backups and CSV exports through the app. These downloaded files may contain personal, academic, and financial information. You are responsible for keeping exported files secure and deciding whether to share them. Importing a backup loads its data into the browser.

7. Storage, deletion, and retention

Your local records generally remain until you delete them, clear browser/site storage, or the browser/device removes its data. The in-app Reset all data control clears app records but does not delete the local login verifier. To erase both records and the local account, clear the site's stored data through your browser settings. Deleting site data may also remove offline files. Download a backup first if needed.

8. Security and limitations

Use a trusted device and browser profile, lock your device, and keep backups private. A local password is a convenience gate and is not a substitute for server-side access control or encrypted storage. The application does not currently offer remote account deletion or cross-device syncing.

9. Changes and questions

This notice may change when the app's features or data handling change. If a person or organization deploys a modified copy or uses the app for other people's records, that operator should publish its own accurate privacy notice with suitable contact information and applicable legal disclosures. For questions about a particular deployed site, contact that site's operator. Avoid posting private attendance or payment details in public issue trackers.

Important: This is an application privacy notice, not a guarantee of compliance with every privacy law. Review and customize it for your deployment, especially if collecting information about other people.
